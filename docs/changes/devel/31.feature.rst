Added ``scripts/run_asr_paper_validation.py``, a paper-driven validation
harness that sweeps the ASR cutoff parameter on real EEG recordings, drops
obviously bad channels, and reproduces the headline figures from Chang et al.
(2020), Blum et al. (2019), and Kim et al. (2025): cutoff sweep
(% windows-with-rejection, % background-variance reduced), per-variant
computation time, reference-data fraction, PSD 1/f-bump parameter, and
frontal eye-blink amplitude reduction.
