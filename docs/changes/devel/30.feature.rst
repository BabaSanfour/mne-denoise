**Spectrum interpolation**:
- Added ``mne_denoise.spectrum_interpolation.SpectrumInterpolation``, a frequency-domain power-line noise remover implementing the spectrum-interpolation method of Leske & Dalal (2019) (a translation of FieldTrip's ``ft_preproc_dftfilter`` spectrum-interpolation mode). It replaces the amplitude of a thin band around the line frequency and its harmonics with an interpolation of the neighbouring amplitudes while preserving the phase, and works with MNE ``Raw``, ``Epochs`` and ``Evoked`` objects as well as NumPy arrays. The lower-level ``interpolate_spectrum`` function is also exposed (Issue #30).
